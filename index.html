        Reading the fight

        • Red-orange is a warning. It marks where an attack will land. When it fills and flashes, the attack fires.
        • Dash through an attack at the last moment for a perfect dodge. Time slows and your Starlight fills.
        • Starlight fills as you land hits. Spend a segment on your hero's special, or hold Mend while standing to restore one health.
        • Every third attack is a finisher. It hits harder than the first two together, and a dash does not break the chain.
        • With the sword or maul, strike downward in the air to bounce off the Warden and get your jump and dash back.
        • The core takes extra damage. Fill the stagger bar to bring it down within reach.
        • A hand that goes dim and cracked is stuck. That is your opening.
        • A run starts with a hero and one of three relics. Each victory grants a boon and leads to a harder trial. Falling ends the run.
