        Reading the fight

        • Red-orange is a warning. It marks where an attack will land; when it fills and flashes, it fires.
        • Dash through an attack at the last moment for a perfect dodge. Your next hit is a Riposte for double.
        • Starlight fills as you land hits. Spend a segment on your special, or hold Mend to restore one health.
        • Every third attack is a finisher. The first one on a staggered boss is a Sunder for more than double.
        • Strike downward in the air with the sword or maul to bounce off a boss.
        • When a boss wards itself and a ring closes on it, break the ward before the ring shuts.
        • Dawnbreak fills as you deal damage. One press when it is full; nothing can touch you while it plays.
        • Flow climbs from C to SS as you hit and dodge without being hit. Each rank adds 3% damage.
        • The Vigil is your camp: echoes change hero, the Shrine spends Sunshards, the brazier sets difficulty, the ring is for practice, the reliquary holds the relic for your run, the gate begins it.
