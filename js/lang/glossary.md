# Solfall: Japanese glossary and style (v3.1a)

Every Japanese string in the game follows this file, so names and terms read the same everywhere.

## Style

- **Natural, native game Japanese**, the way a Japanese action game or roguelite (Hades, Dead Cells, Hollow Knight
  JP) would say it. Never word-for-word from English. Rephrase freely so it reads as if written in Japanese first.
- **UI and descriptions:** short and plain. Use 体言止め or plain form (〜する／〜になる). No です／ます in
  descriptions, tips or labels. Example: 「回復で体力が2回復する。」, 「星光が満タンの間、与ダメージ+25%。」
- **Button and menu labels:** short verbs or nouns: 「続きから」「設定」「野営地へ戻る」「恩恵を選ぶ」.
- **Prompts shown in the camp** ("use: take up X's bow") use a short verb phrase: 「手に取る」「話す」「調べる」.
- **Boss titles and dramatic lines** (taunts, cards, the epilogue) may be literary and terse: 「なおも立ち上がる」.
- **Numbers** stay as Arabic digits. Roman numerals for trials and phases stay (試練IV, フェーズII).
- **Damage and percentages:** 「40ダメージ」「与ダメージ+25%」「50%増し」.
- Keep **{0}, {1}...** placeholders exactly; move them wherever Japanese word order needs them.
- Keep **HTML tags** (`<b>`, `<span ...>`) intact around the right words.
- **Key names** (Space, Shift, E, LB, RT...) stay as they are.
- No full-width spaces for layout. Use 「・」 for the "·" separator (or keep " · " where it separates fields).
